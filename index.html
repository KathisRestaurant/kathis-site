OUR STORY

AMERICAN-INDIAN WRAPS

We keep the menu tight and do it properly. Our Indian Kathis are the real thing: tandoori chicken, malai tikka, beef seekh kabab, roasted and spiced the traditional way. Our American Kathis take the flavors everyone already loves, from buffalo chicken to Philly steak, and roll them the same way. One flaky paratha, made fresh to order, every time.

OUR SIGNATURE THE RECTANGULAR PARATHA Our paratha is rolled rectangular, not round. It wraps into a tight, even Kathi with filling from end to end, and you'll know a Kathis the moment you see one.

WE'RE HIRING

CAREERS AT KATHIS

No tandoor or chef background needed. Every role is trained on our recipe and time/temperature cards. Start as a Team Member, learn the line, and grow into running the store.

LEVEL 1 $18/hr

Team Member

Front counter: taking orders on the POS, packing orders, drinks and keeping the store clean.

WHAT YOU NEED
  • No experience needed; we train you in one day
  • California Food Handler Card within 30 days of hire
  • Friendly with customers, comfortable with basic math
  • On your feet for a full shift; able to lift 25 lb
LEVEL 2 $20/hr

Kathi Maker / Prep Cook

Cooks the line (griddle, charbroiler, oven), makes our paratha in batches, marinates and portions proteins, makes gravy and chutneys, and handles receiving and inventory counts.

WHAT YOU NEED
  • 3+ months as a Team Member, or 6 months of kitchen/line experience
  • California Food Handler Card
  • 18 or older (to run kitchen equipment)
  • Follows recipe and time/temperature cards with probe-thermometer logs
  • Able to lift 50 lb; passes our line and paratha sign-off
LEVEL 3 $23/hr

Store Manager / Shift Lead

Runs shifts and cash, trains new hires, builds the schedule, orders and receives stock, keeps food and labor costs on target, and handles health inspections.

WHAT YOU NEED
  • 1+ year as a Kathi Maker, or 1–2 years as a shift lead in quick service
  • California Food Protection Manager certificate (e.g. ServSafe Manager)
  • Cash handling; basic POS and spreadsheet skills
  • Able to train others

All roles are hourly at Yuba Sutter Marketplace, Yuba City. Pay shown is the starting base rate. Overtime follows California rules: 1.5× over 8 hours a day or 40 a week, 2× over 12 hours a day.
