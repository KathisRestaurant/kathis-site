AMERICAN-INDIAN WRAPS

We keep the menu tight and do it properly. Our Indian Kathis are the real thing: butter chicken, malai tikka, tandoori, char-grilled and spiced the traditional way. Our American Kathis take the flavors everyone already loves, from buffalo chicken to Philly steak, and roll them the same way. One flaky paratha, made fresh to order, every time.
