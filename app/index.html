GETTING STARTED

SIGN IN WITH JUST YOUR EMAIL

  1. Open the Kathis app. It works in your phone's web browser or on a computer. iPhone and Android versions are on the way.
  2. Enter your email. No password to remember. We email you a 6-digit code from Kathi's Wraps.
  3. Type in the code. That's it. Your account is ready, with your own member code and 0 points to start.

You can look at the menu and build a bag without signing in. You'll sign in when you check out.

ORDERING

FROM PHONE TO PICKUP

  1. Pick your Kathis. Tap any item to add it to your bag. Make any Kathi a combo with fries and a fountain drink for $5 more.
  2. Choose a pickup time. "As soon as possible" is usually about 10 minutes, or pick a time in 15-minute steps. When we're closed you can schedule for the next day we're open. Online orders stop 15 minutes before closing.
  3. Pay in the app. Pay by card, and save it for next time if you like. Sales tax is added at checkout. Card payments are handled by Square; we never see or store your full card number.
  4. Pick it up. Your order screen shows an order number and updates as we go: received, preparing, ready. When it says ready, show the number at the Kathis counter in the food court.

Your order history lives in Account. Open any past order to see what you had, what you paid and the points you earned, or tap Reorder to put the same items in a new bag.

HOW TO GET IT

  1. Request it in the app. Go to Account, find Request change of status, and tap Request next to the one that fits.
  2. Get verified. Next time you're at the counter, open Account and show your member code (it looks like KW-AB12CD) and your proof, like your mall badge.
  3. Save on every app order. Once approved, the discount applies automatically.

Member discounts apply to orders placed in the app. You can cancel a waiting request, or go back to regular prices, from Account at any time. Kathis team members get their perks automatically when they sign in with their Kathis work email.

SIGN OUT

Use Sign out in the top bar or at the bottom of Account. Sign back in anytime with a new email code.

Deleting your account: go to Account, tap Delete my account at the bottom, and confirm. This removes your sign-in, your points, your status and your saved cards right away, and it can't be undone. We keep a record of past orders for our books, with your name removed. You're welcome to join again later with the same email, starting fresh.

QUESTIONS

GOOD TO KNOW

Do I earn points when I order at the register?

Not yet. Points, deals and member discounts are for orders placed in the app.

I didn't get my sign-in code.

Check your spam or promotions folder for an email from Kathi's Wraps. Codes expire after an hour, so request a new one if it's been a while.

Can I order when you're closed?

Yes. Choose a pickup time on the next day we're open. Same-day orders stop 15 minutes before closing.

What if my order is canceled?

If you were charged, the refund goes back to your card. Any points you spent on a reward come back to your account.

Can I use a deal and my member discount together?

No, they don't stack. Your bag picks whichever saves you more.

Something looks wrong with my order.

Come to the counter with your order number and we'll sort it out.
